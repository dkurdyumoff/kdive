About Us

Kris, Dima & Moe

We are a team that organises unique diving safaris in the Red Sea.

We are instructors, divers, and simply in love with the ocean. Our instructors are PADI professionals from the well-known Blue Sea Dive Club.

We believe that a diving safari is more than just dives — it's a complete immersion into an atmosphere of adventure, discovery, and unforgettable experiences. We take care of every detail so you can fully relax and enjoy every moment.

For us, this isn't just a business — it's a passion project. We put a piece of ourselves into every tour to make it something truly special and memorable for a lifetime.

Format

What is a Diving Safari?

A journey both above and beneath the surface

01

Exploring the Most Stunning Reefs

The boat takes you to sites unreachable from shore — reefs, wrecks, and protected marine areas.

02

Snorkelling with Dolphins

The Red Sea is one of the rare places where dolphins approach on their own — right up to the boat.

03

BBQ on Deck & Island Bonfires

Dinner at anchor, a sky full of stars above and a fire on white sand.

04

Great Company

Groups of up to 12 people — strangers become friends fast. Life on a liveaboard brings people together.

05

Learn to Dive

PADI courses right on board. Start from scratch and earn your certification during the trip.

06

And Simply Rest

Sun deck, a good book, full board, Wi-Fi, zero deadlines. A real holiday.
