FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What courses are available?

PADI courses available at sea:

  • PADI Open Water Diver — for beginners. Get your full diving certification during the safari.
  • PADI Refresh Course — certified but haven't dived in a while? Refresh your skills.
  • PADI Specialties (EAN · Deep · Wreck) — for certified divers: nitrox, deep and wreck dives.
  • PADI Advanced OWD — next level: sharpen your skills and earn the Advanced certification.
Who are the dive instructors?

Our instructors are PADI professionals with 20+ years of experience. We partner with the renowned Blue Sea Dive Club (Sharm El-Sheikh).

How safe is it?

Diving is far safer than most people think. Modern equipment, professional instructors and strict safety protocols make it one of the most controlled adventure activities.

Can I travel solo?

Yes, most of our guests travel solo. A dive safari is a great way to meet like-minded people — the yacht brings everyone together quickly.

Do I need diving experience?

No, not at all. Beginners can join the Discovery Dive programme — introductory dives with an instructor in safe conditions.

What certification do I need?

Independent diving requires an Open Water Diver certification or above. No cert? You can complete the Open Water course on board, or simply enjoy snorkelling and Discovery Dives.

Haven't dived in a long time?

No problem! We offer a Refresh Course — a quick theory review and skills practice with an instructor before the safari begins.

What should I pack?

Pack light summer clothing, swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, a first-aid kit, a GoPro and seasickness tablets just in case.

Can I rent equipment?

Yes, all essential dive gear (mask, fins, wetsuit, BCD, regulator) is available for hire on board. Please let us know your rental needs in advance.

What if the weather is bad?

September is an excellent month for Red Sea diving. In unforeseen circumstances, the captain will adjust the route to alternative, sheltered sites.

How do I book a spot?

Submit an enquiry → we contact you to confirm details → sign the agreement → you pay the deposit.

What is the deposit and when is the balance due?

The deposit is 50% of the tour cost. The remaining balance is due 30 days before the safari starts.

What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation 60+ days before — 80% refund. 30–60 days — 50%. Less than 30 days — deposit non-refundable.
